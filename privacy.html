Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information SJI DEV (the "Service"), operated by SJI ("we", "us"), collects, how it is used and protected, and the choices you have. It applies to the people who sign in to the Service and to the owners of the social media accounts connected to it.

1. Information we collect

We do not request or collect TikTok followers, messages, viewing history, contacts or analytics. We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

2. How information is used

  • To publish the content that authorized users schedule, to the accounts they choose.
  • To display account names, posting options and publishing results inside the Service.
  • To keep the Service secure and working.

We only publish content that a signed-in user has created and scheduled. We never post anything to a connected account on our own initiative.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared only as needed to run the Service:

  • TikTok and Meta (Facebook and Instagram): the post content and settings are sent to the platform you choose, under that platform's own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare: photos sent to TikTok may be stored briefly in Cloudflare R2 so TikTok can download them; they are deleted once TikTok has processed the post. Cloudflare Workers AI may be used to generate images when the user asks for it.
  • AI text providers (such as Groq or Cloudflare Workers AI): only when a user asks the Service to write a caption or article; the related text and media are sent to generate it.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

4. Storage and security

  • Data is stored on a server controlled by SJI.
  • Platform access tokens are encrypted (AES-GCM) before they are stored. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes.
  • Only signed-in, authorized SJI users can use the Service, and actions are recorded with the user who performed them.

5. Retention

  • TikTok profile information and tokens are kept while the account is connected and deleted when it is disconnected.
  • Post records and uploaded media are kept for the team's posting history and can be deleted by an administrator.
  • Temporary copies of photos sent to TikTok are deleted after publishing.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect TikTok: on the "My account" page of the Service, click Disconnect. You can also remove the app's access in your TikTok app settings, under the apps connected to your account.
  • Access or deletion: email prg1.saptajayasejati@gmail.com to ask what information we hold about you or to have it deleted. We will reply within 30 days.

7. Children

The Service is an internal business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
