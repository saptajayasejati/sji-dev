SJI DEV

A social media post scheduling tool operated by SJI

SJI DEV is a web application used by SJI's team to prepare and schedule posts for the social media accounts that SJI manages. Authorized team members write a caption, attach a photo or video, choose the accounts and a publishing time, and the post is published automatically at that time.

Supported platforms

  • Facebook Pages and the Instagram professional accounts linked to them, through Meta's official APIs.
  • TikTok, through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

How TikTok posting works

  1. The owner of a TikTok account connects it by signing in with TikTok and approving the requested permissions. The account can be disconnected at any time.
  2. A team member writes a caption, attaches a video or photo, sees a preview, chooses the TikTok account and picks a time.
  3. At that time, SJI DEV sends the content in one of two ways chosen for each post:
    • As a draft (default): the video or photo is uploaded to the creator's TikTok inbox. The creator receives a notification in the TikTok app, where they edit it, choose who can view it and decide whether to post it. Nothing becomes visible until the creator posts it in TikTok.
    • Direct post: before scheduling, the person creating the post chooses who can view it (from the options TikTok allows for that account), whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, and whether the post promotes a brand, and agrees to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation. The post is then published with exactly those settings.
  4. The status of every post is shown in SJI DEV. TikTok may take a few minutes to process content after it is sent.

SJI DEV never posts on its own initiative, never adds watermarks or promotional branding to content, and only sends content that a signed-in team member has created and scheduled.

Who can use it

SJI DEV is an internal tool used by SJI. Only people who have been given an account by an SJI administrator can sign in, and only accounts that their owners have connected and authorized can be posted to. There is no public sign-up.
