SJI DEV

A social media post scheduling tool operated by SJI

SJI DEV is a web application used by SJI's team to prepare and schedule posts for the social media accounts that SJI manages. Authorized team members write a caption, attach a photo or video, choose the accounts and a publishing time, and the post is published automatically at that time.

Supported platforms

  • Facebook Pages and the Instagram professional accounts linked to them, through Meta's official APIs.
  • TikTok, through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. Before each TikTok post, the person creating it chooses who can view the post, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, and whether the post promotes a brand, and confirms TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation.

Who can use it

SJI DEV is an internal tool. Only people who have been given an account by an SJI administrator can sign in, and only accounts that their owners have connected and authorized can be posted to. There is no public sign-up.
